# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Malaysia Transit Live serves two read-only audiences: commuters checking current service coverage and vehicle positions, and operators monitoring feed health and freshness. Commuters are often scanning on a phone or shared screen; operators need a dense, dependable view on a desktop monitor.

## Product Purpose

Provide a trustworthy public view of Malaysian transit services by combining scheduled GTFS data, validated Vehicle Positions, source health, and clear freshness semantics. Success means a user can find a mode, operator, or city, understand whether positions are available, and inspect a vehicle without confusing a timetable with an ETA.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, civic.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI dashboards, purple palettes, gradients, glassmorphism, decorative metric cards, ambiguous status colors, noisy motion, and operational claims that are not supported by the upstream data.

## Design Principles

- Make data provenance and freshness visible at the point of use.
- Preserve an equivalent list path for every map interaction.
- Separate commuter discovery from operational health without duplicating filters or data semantics.
- Prefer familiar, dense product controls over decorative novelty.
- Never imply an arrival prediction when the source only provides a vehicle position.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, text resizing, dark mode, reduced motion, color-vision differences, responsive layouts from 375px upward, and explicit text labels for every status and icon-only action.
